# Milestone 4 — Final Affidavit Structure

**Required deliverable:** comprehensive forensic affidavit for the High
Court, plus a 15-minute mock expert testimony/presentation.

## Affidavit structure

1. Court and case identification
2. Examiner qualifications and role
3. Authority, scope and questions examined
4. Evidence inventory and chain of custody
5. Methods and validated tools
6. Package A validation findings
7. Package B acquisition and analysis findings
8. Cross-domain correlation and master timeline
9. Alternative hypotheses and limitations
10. Conclusions stated at the forensic — not legal — level
11. Declaration of truthfulness and signature/date

## Executive forensic conclusion — how to frame it

The course's capstone walkthrough anticipates a mixed-authenticity
finding: the leak is not treated as wholly genuine. The anticipated
scenario includes audio splicing, manipulated PDF/financial content, and
evidence on the seized laptop suggesting AI-tool activity and access to
incriminating files.

**This is a study aid, not a substitute for the real result.** The
examiner must still present findings as evidence-based, drawn only from
the actual supplied files, state limitations and confidence levels, and
avoid the legal conclusion that a suspect is simply "guilty" — that
determination belongs to the court, not the forensic report.
